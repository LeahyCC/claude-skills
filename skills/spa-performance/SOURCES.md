## Platform facts used

- A long task is main-thread work over 50 ms. [Optimize long tasks](https://web.dev/articles/optimize-long-tasks).
- A good INP is 200 ms or under, from interaction to the next paint. [INP](https://web.dev/articles/inp).
- A long animation frame is a render update delayed past 50 ms. LoAF shipped in Chrome 123 and attributes scripts. Long tasks stop before render and often name no script. [Long Animation Frames](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/web-platform/long-animation-frames).
- `scheduler.yield()` continues at the front of similar tasks. `setTimeout(0)` goes to the back. Nested timers clamp to 4 ms after the fifth nesting. [MDN yield()](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Scheduler/yield), [MDN setTimeout](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Window/setTimeout#nested_timeouts).
- `navigator.scheduling.isInputPending()` is deprecated. It can return false when input is waiting, and animation matters too. [MDN isInputPending()](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Scheduling/isInputPending).
- `content-visibility: auto` skips contents that are not relevant to the user, and still allows find-in-page and tab order. The relevancy rule is in [draw-less.md](./resources/draw-less.md).

## Worked example

The harness rules were checked against a tldraw PDF canvas measured on 2026-10-22 (headed Chromium, 1400 by 900 at 2x, `--enable-precise-memory-info`). The numbers in [measure.md](./resources/measure.md) are that app on that machine. They show that one optimization alone can lose on the gesture. They are not a budget for other apps.
