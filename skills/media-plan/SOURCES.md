## Creators' own process

What Gossip Goblin has said in public differs from this skill's route, and the skill does not claim to copy it. From PJ Ace's [interview write-up](https://pjace.beehiiv.com/p/gossip-goblin-s-crazy-workflow-for-building-original-worlds-200m-views) (May 2026), [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/may/14/gossip-goblin-ai-film-making-new-era-hollywood) (May 2026) and [Film Threat](https://filmthreat.com/features/the-first-ai-animated-feature-film-in-theaters-gods-dont-give-gifts-director-zack-london-talks-about-the-future-of-cinema/) (November 2026): stills come first, from hosted image tools, and every shot is animated from a still in hosted video tools; lines are spoken by real voice actors; there is no single master character sheet, but separate close, mid, wide and rear shots of each character; a team prompts each performance many times. Nothing found says how he storyboards or how many takes he makes. This skill keeps the parts that hold on a local setup (stills first, image to video, many takes, sound last) and adds the master-still rule because local edit models hold a character better than fresh prompts.

## Not claimed

- No plan from this skill has been made into a finished video yet. The worked example is a plan, not a tested piece.
- "Krea 2 Turbo looked the most real", "Qwen 2512 places big scale better, then a Krea refine", "a named medium reads as a photo", "mixed lights look rendered", the pocket watch lid, and Lightning giving calmer motion come from runs on one local ComfyUI setup on 2026-11-22 and 23, judged by eye. They are not vendor claims.
- "LTX-2.5 makes a new voice each clip" is inferred: nothing in Lightricks' docs ties a voice to a character across separate generations. Check it on a first run.
- The hook in 1 to 2 seconds is this skill's target. TikTok's ad guide says 6 seconds for the hook and 3 for the message; Meta says "the first few seconds"; the 1 to 3 second figure comes from creator blogs.
- Beats per length and shots per length are worked out from the models' clip lengths. No story or platform source gives a count.
- Cut lengths of 2 to 4 seconds and loops come from creator blogs, not studies.
- Meta's 35% bottom band is from its ad specs. Top and side percentages often quoted elsewhere were not found on a Meta page. TikTok and YouTube publish no fixed zone for ordinary posts.
- -14 LUFS for social video is convention: Spotify publishes it; YouTube's figure is measured by engineers; TikTok and Instagram publish none.
- Several StudioBinder pages, the ACX page and the StudentFilmmakers article returned errors and were read from search summaries. Their rows say so. Re-read before quoting.
- Hands and small props as a failure come from real runs and media-prompt's fixes.md. No vendor doc found names them.
- No source recommends a LoRA for character consistency, so the skill does not.
